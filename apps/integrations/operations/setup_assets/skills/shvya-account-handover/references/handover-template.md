# Account handover template

Replace every planning label with sourced content before delivery. Omit a section only when explicitly not applicable and explain why.

1. Business and objective: organization, audience, outcome, timezone, scope and date.
2. Evidence/readiness: current revision, readback, validators, engine tests, live delivery and remaining limits.
3. Lead journey per pipeline: stage/event definition, human or AI owner, entry/exit, diagram and blocked paths.
4. Qualification: ordered questions, required/optional gate, stored attributes/descriptions and final destination.
5. AI Setup: supported languages, persona disclosure, knowledge sources, disclosure rules, human handoff and opt-out.
6. Files and FAQs: actual available sources, ingestion status, share_when rules and unresolved material.
7. Cadences: purpose, segment, exact channel/sender, approved template/text mode, timing anchor, stop/restart policy and enrollment status.
8. Workflows: trigger, conditions, bound action, activation, dependencies and verification.
9. Quick Replies: situations/categories with evidence-backed usage guidance.
10. Calendar/reminders: what is recorded, what is booked, owner and confirmation evidence.
11. Demo guide: Send / Expect / Explain / Check / Evidence, one observable outcome per step.
12. Operations: monitoring views, edit owners, approved commitments, escalation and how to report a defect with IDs.
13. Gaps/decisions: issue, source, risk, required action, owner and real or unspecified deadline.
14. Acceptance: what is ready now, what was tested, cleanup and the next authorized step.

Never include credentials, internal hidden reasoning, unrelated client details, unapproved commercial promises or invented exact replies. Explain unsupported features candidly.
