# Industry designer domain checks

## Evidence
Use verified business model, target customer, sales motion, lead sources, cycle length, qualification gate, channels, languages, appointment needs and compliance constraints. Compare against current tenant configuration before proposing a blueprint.

## Known traps
- Industry defaults are not business facts.
- Similar businesses can have different qualification gates and stage semantics.
- A recommended stage list can duplicate/proliferate existing stages.
- A “best practice” sequence may be inappropriate for the client's sales cycle or channel consent.
- Vertical examples may contain offers, prices, proof or terminology that must never transfer to another company.

## Verification
Mark each proposed element reuse/adapt/create/defer, name source/assumption, and attach an acceptance criterion. Validate the blueprint against live capability discovery before handing off.

## Handoffs
CRM structure → CRM architect; qualification → qualification; AI facts/behavior → AI Brain; automation → Workflow/Cadence; channels → routing/provider skill; final readiness → acceptance testing.
