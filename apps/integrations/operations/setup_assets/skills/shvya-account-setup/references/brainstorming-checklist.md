# Company setup intake checklist

Use existing materials first. Ask a compact batch only for facts that change the build; mark the rest as gaps and proceed with independent work. This captures the substantive fourteen areas in the source process using Shvya terms. A transcript is valuable but not a prerequisite for honest, limited work.

## 1. Business context

Company name/identity; one-line purpose; actual products/services and scope; buyer types; verified differentiators; locations/service area; languages and scripts; hours/timezone; approved website, contacts and materials; prohibited claims; regulated/sensitive topics and human handoff boundaries.

## 2. Lead sources and volume

List each actual source, volume, metadata and origin; separate lead generation from a connected ingestion path. Capture WhatsApp click-to-chat, social ads, website, marketplaces, CRM, Sheets, referrals and imports only if used. For migration record source, mapping, duplication policy and owner; do not import without authorization. Do not invent ad-tracking fields or claim a connector exists.

## 3. Qualification

Define qualified in one sentence. Capture required versus optional fields; per-product branches; exact/natural question wording; allowed answers and numeric units; disqualification versus soft timing objections; early price questions; acknowledgment; human request and escalation; opt-out response. Confirm pricing disclosure and each hard gate's evidence.

## 4. Funnel and pipelines

List real business events from new enquiry to outcome; stage names/descriptions; AI versus human ownership; terminal states; non-response, dormant, ineligible and do-not-contact handling. Record multiple pipelines only when needed. Each sender's pipeline mapping must be explicit. Discover protected Shvya stages rather than assuming legacy defaults.

## 5. Attributes

For each field: purpose, display name, type, options/units, requirement ID, required status, extraction evidence, source, sensitivity and retention needs. Reuse existing identity fields and canonical lead source where applicable. Verify current platform quotas rather than legacy plan limits.

## 6. Cadences

Per Cadence: audience, entry trigger, intent, channel/provider/sender, message assets, relative delays and cumulative offsets, timezone/business hours, stop events, expiry and re-entry. Record any attachments and whether delivery is supported. Confirm email transport if used. Do not invent treatment outcomes, scarcity or promotional offers to fill slots.

## 7. Workflows

Record event -> stage/source/attribute conditions -> action, one action per proposed Workflow, with expected overlap and ordering. Determine how to stop outreach, route silence, end recovery, hand off, handle replies and avoid loops. Round robin, notifications and provider-specific actions remain capability questions, not assumed enum values.

## 8. WhatsApp/channel decision

Capture current connection type, exact number/account, owning pipeline, readiness, API template requirements, health, supported channels and desired migration. Keep secrets out of the transcript and kit. Connection/QR/OAuth work follows the appropriate Shvya flow; do not ask for admin passwords or raw access tokens in chat.

## 9. Team and handoff

Named or role-based owner for each pipeline and escalation; team hours; intended response expectation; existing member access; responsibilities. User creation, invites and permission changes are separate work if not supported by exposed MCP. Do not promise a callback time or SLA not committed by the team.

## 10. Integrations

Every source from section 2 gets a row: source, destination, flow direction, field mapping, connection state, in-scope/deferred/unsupported, owner, target date or none given, acceptance test. Record secure setup action, not credentials. Distinguish a provider read/auth check from a working end-to-end lead import.

## 11. Knowledge and assets

Collect brochures, policies, catalogues, price lists, website pages, approved case studies and FAQ answers. Record provenance, version/effective date and allowed use. Separate retrieval documents from assets that can actually be sent. A knowledge ingestion manifest is not proof of publication; a filename is not a delivery ID.

## 12. Plan, usage and billing

Record the client's actual selected plan, expected usage, billing owner and required limits only if supplied/currently verified. Do not copy the source company's plan names, prices, trial duration, quota numbers or credit consumption. Billing changes require their own supported process and authorization.

## 13. Success measures

Capture agreed 30/60/90-day goals, baseline where known, qualification/response/conversion measures, owner and review cadence. Goals are client targets, not Shvya guarantees. Record requested reporting integrations as tasks.

## 14. Commitments

For each promise made on the call: exact words, source locator, call date, promised deadline or `none given`, owner or `unassigned`, status, dependency and evidence of completion. Include setup dates, integrations, migration, training, channel changes and all material "we will" promises. Do not invent deadlines or delete commitments that need renegotiation.

No Shvya Ops CRM task-write endpoint was verified in this kit's MCP surface. Keep the commitment ledger local and hand it to the responsible team; use another authorized task connector only when explicitly in scope. Do not copy legacy webhook URLs or token handling.

## End-of-intake deliverables

Source inventory; profile/fact ledger; preferences; Playbook requirements and branches; stages/attributes; Cadence outline; Workflow grid; source/integration map; knowledge manifest; channel/team plan; commitments; gaps and acceptance criteria. Populate what is known and assign open questions. Do not require all fourteen sections to be fully answered before producing useful drafts.
