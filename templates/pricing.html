Which plan is right for you?

A few quick answers before you pick.

Essentials or Growth Lab — how do I decide? ▾
It comes down to how many products or services you sell.
  • One product, and you want a ready-to-run system without overthinking it — Industry Essentials.
  • Multiple products, each needing its own bot and sequences — Growth Lab.
Is DIY actually enough to get results? ▾
DIY gives you the full CRM and lead tracking, but you're building and maintaining the AI agent, sequences, and integrations yourself. It works if you have the time and technical comfort for that. Most teams that want a faster, done-for-you result start with Essentials instead.
How long does setup take? ▾
Industry Essentials is typically built and live in 7–10 working days, including your AI agent, sequences, pipeline, and onboarding calls. Growth Lab takes a bit longer since it covers multiple product lines — we'll give you a timeline before you commit.
Will my team need training? ▾
Not much, on Essentials or Growth Lab — the system is built and handed to your team, who mostly work from the pipeline and tap to send replies. Onboarding calls cover the rest. DIY needs more upfront time since your team configures the flows themselves.
