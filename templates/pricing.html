Pricing FAQ

Pick the plan by how much setup you want to own

The software is only part of the decision. The bigger difference is who configures the sales workflow.

Done for You is the practical middle option when you want the CRM, AI qualification, follow-up structure, and onboarding configured without paying for deeper Enterprise customization. DIY is better if you genuinely want to build the setup yourself.

SHVYA helps configure the core qualification logic, business knowledge, follow-up sequences, lead structure, and onboarding around your sales process. Your team still runs the conversations and closes the deals, but it does not start from an empty workspace.

Choose Enterprise when you have multiple product lines, more complex qualification logic, advanced behaviour-based automation, or custom integrations that need a deeper implementation than the standard Done for You setup.

Yes. The three plans are designed around different levels of setup and complexity, so you can begin with a simpler plan and move to a more involved implementation as your sales operation grows.
