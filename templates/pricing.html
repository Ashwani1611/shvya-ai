BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

A few useful answers.

What does Done for You cover?+

Done for You includes everything in DIY plus Instagram Automation, SHVYA Calendar, guided onboarding, and configuration of core qualification, business knowledge, follow-ups, lead capture, pipelines and workflows around your sales process.

When should I choose Enterprise?+

Enterprise is designed for sales teams that want the complete managed sales operation: everything in Done for You plus SHVYA Sales, Call Intelligence, advanced sales automation, deeper team intelligence, integrations and priority implementation.

Are AI Coins different across plans?+

No. DIY, Done for You and Enterprise each include 5,000 AI Coins. The plan difference is based on product capabilities, implementation depth and service.

Can I start with DIY?+

Yes. DIY is designed for teams comfortable configuring their own workflow. Discuss a more supported implementation when your needs change.

Is this a free trial?+

The existing policy describes a seven-day evaluation period after paid deployment. It is not a free trial. Refund eligibility and exclusions are set out in the Refund Policy.
