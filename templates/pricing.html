BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

A few useful answers.

What does Done for You cover?+

Shvya configures core qualification logic, business knowledge, follow-up sequences, lead capture and onboarding around your sales process. Your team runs the conversations and makes the sales decisions.

When should I choose Enterprise?+

Choose Enterprise when you need multi-product qualification, advanced behaviour-based follow-up, custom integrations or a more involved rollout.

Can I start with DIY?+

Yes. DIY is designed for teams comfortable configuring their own workflow. Discuss a more supported implementation when your needs change.

Is this a free trial?+

The existing policy describes a seven-day evaluation period after paid deployment. It is not a free trial. Refund eligibility and exclusions are set out in the Refund Policy.
