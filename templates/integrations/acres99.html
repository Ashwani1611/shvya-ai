CONNECTION

How leads arrive

99acres offers two documented XML exchange methods.

Push API99acres → SHVYA
Webhook

99acres sends batches of buyer enquiries to your unique organization endpoint. Successful query IDs are acknowledged individually.

Pull APISHVYA → 99acres
Scheduled

SHVYA requests enquiries using securely stored account credentials. Scheduled polling respects the documented 6 requests/hour limit.

99acres controls Push delivery timing. Their supplied document describes hourly batches; the actual cadence depends on your provider account.
