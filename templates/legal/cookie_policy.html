1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites recognize your device, remember your preferences, improve your browsing experience, analyze website performance, and personalize content and ads.

Cookies cannot access files on your device, harm your system, or collect personal information without your consent.

2. How We Use Cookies

A. Essential & Functional Cookies

These enable core features of the website, such as page navigation, secure login (if applicable), form submissions, and site preferences. Without these cookies, the website may not function properly.

B. Analytics & Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use the website so we can improve it. They may track which pages are visited, time spent on each page, errors or crashes, and traffic sources. We may use tools like Google Analytics for this purpose.

C. Preference Cookies

These remember your language settings, display preferences, and previously selected options — helping provide a smoother, more personalised experience.

D. Marketing & Third-Party Cookies

These are used (only if applicable) to show relevant ads, track ad performance, and measure campaign impact. Examples include Meta Pixel, Google Ads, etc. You will only encounter marketing cookies if we run ads or tracking scripts on that specific page.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may come from third-party providers integrated into our website, such as:

  • Google (Analytics, reCAPTCHA)
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram Pixel)
  • WhatsApp widget
  • Payment gateway services
  • Embedded third-party content (videos, forms, etc.)

These third parties control their own cookies and their own privacy policies apply. Shvya does not control or access third-party cookie data.

4. Personal Data & Cookies

Some cookies may collect personal data such as device information, IP address, browser type, session identifiers, and interaction data. Any personal data collected through cookies is processed according to the Shvya Privacy Policy.

5. How to Manage or Disable Cookies

You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

You can block all cookies, allow only essential cookies, clear existing cookies, or block third-party cookies.

Note: If you disable cookies, some features of the Shvya website may no longer work properly.

By continuing to use the site after seeing the notice, you consent to our use of cookies as described. You may withdraw your consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings.
