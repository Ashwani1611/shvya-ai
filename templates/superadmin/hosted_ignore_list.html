How this protection works

Sync takes a snapshot of direct chats already present in every currently connected Hosted Account. Their names and phone numbers are stored here. Future live messages from these numbers are still saved in WhatsApp Chats, but they cannot automatically create a new CRM lead. If your team manually creates a lead for one of these numbers, normal lead-based AI engagement can continue for that lead.

Resetting the list removes that protection. After reset, a future live message from a previously ignored number may create a lead automatically when Auto Lead Creation is enabled. You can build a fresh snapshot at any time by clicking Sync all existing chats again.
