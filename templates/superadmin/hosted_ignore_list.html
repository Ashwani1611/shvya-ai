How protection works

The snapshot changes only when a Superadmin syncs or resets it.

Existing chat

If the number is in this list and has no CRM lead, an incoming message is stored but no lead is auto-created and AI does not engage.

New chat

A number that is not in this snapshot follows the Hosted Account's normal auto-lead and AI settings.

Manual intervention

If a CRM lead is manually created for an ignored number, future messages attach to that lead and normal AI rules may run.
