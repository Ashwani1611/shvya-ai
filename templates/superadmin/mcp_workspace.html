Security

                
Controls enforced below the client and model layer.

            

        

        

            
Credentials: hashed at rest, short-lived OAuth access, rotating refresh, expiring direct keys.

            
Tenant boundary: one server-side Superadmin organization context; tool arguments cannot replace it.

            
Writes: capability + live policy + grant snapshot + OAuth scope + dry-run/approval + backend validation.

            
Evidence: safe append-only audits and single-use approval receipts; secrets and customer content are excluded.
